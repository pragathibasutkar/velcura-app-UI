Returns & Exchanges Protocols

Standard operating procedures for individual clinician orders and hospital ward fitting samples.

We understand that healthcare professionals require apparel that performs uncompromised through rigorous 14-hour duty shifts. Individual clinicians may exchange unworn, unwashed scrubs within 14 days of receipt for an alternate size or cut with zero restocking fees. For institutional ward consignments, sample fitting trial pieces may be tested against your facility's 134°C autoclave standards prior to bulk fleet embroidery.

Important Notice: Clinical Hygiene & Non-Returnable Parameters

In strict compliance with Indian medical sanitation guidelines and NABH infection control directives, garments that have undergone personalized doctor caduceus or institutional hospital crest embroidery, individual nametag monogramming, garments worn inside live surgical suites, or garments customized with altered hem lengths cannot be returned for a cash refund. However, sizing trial sets prior to fleet monogramming remain 100% exchangeable.
