## Brand & Style

This design system adapts clinical wellness into a high-trust, B2B institutional client portal engineered specifically for partner hospitals, clinical administrators, and procurement leads. The aesthetic fuses clinical precision with grounded, restorative wellness—subverting cold enterprise software through organic warmth, architectural clarity, and clear clinical signaling.

### Target Audience & Emotional Arc
- **Target Audience:** Hospital procurement directors, chief medical officers, clinical pharmacy heads, and institutional care teams managing continuous bulk formulations, emergency medicine supplies, and clinical logistics.
- **Emotional Tone:** Reassuring, operationally clear, calm, and data-forward. The interface reduces cognitive strain during high-stakes clinical ordering through human-centered plain language, calm visual hierarchy, and instant operational transparency.

### Visual Style
- **Clinical Minimalist Architecture:** A hybrid of institutional reliability and tactile warmth. The UI relies on generous breathing room, crisp white clinical cards framed by quiet sand borders rather than aggressive shadows, and purposeful chromatic anchors.
- **Institutional Authority:** Solid architectural anchors—most notably a commanding Deep Teal sidebar accented by a Plum horizon rule, paired with a crisp utility bar highlighting hospital identity and dedicated account management.

## Colors

The color palette establishes an uncompromising operational hierarchy, balancing the grounding depth of Deep Teal with purposeful secondary accents for metrics and clear semantic status indicators.

### Structural Framework
- **Primary Canvas (`#F7F5EF` - Soft Ivory):** Foundation for all screen backgrounds, reducing optical fatigue during prolonged clinical monitoring.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF` - Crisp White):** Primary card, data table, and modal container surface, framed cleanly against Soft Ivory.
- **Structural Sidebar (`#164E52` - Deep Teal):** The persistent operational anchor, grounding primary system navigation with high-contrast Ivory text and icons.
- **Sidebar Accent Rule (`#5B3A57` - Plum):** A refined 3px–4px structural rule crowning the institutional sidebar, reinforcing institutional gravitas.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#D8CBB8` - Muted Sand):** Quiet 1px containment borders for cards, utility bars, table rows, and neutral inputs. Replaces heavy dropped shadows.

### Core Metrics & Accents
- **Secondary Accent (`#4F7A92` - Dusty Blue):** Institutional KPI icons, metric pill containers, clinical registry counters, and analytics trend markers.
- **Guidance Surface (`#DCE9E1` - Mint Mist):** Background fill for persistent assistance panels, clinical hotline cards, and protocol support notes.
- **Body & Value Typography (`#2B2F2E` - Charcoal):** High-contrast neutral for all UI copy, data values, and transactional headings.

### Operational Semantics
- **Pending & Review (`#E3A72F` - Saffron):** In-transit status, awaiting clinical sign-off, inventory alerts, and primary focus rings. Pair exclusively with Charcoal text for accessibility.
- **Critical Urgency (`#B4552F` - Terracotta):** Order cancellations, critical inventory depletion, cold-chain temperature breaches, and urgent delivery exceptions.
- **Verified & Completed (`#3F7D5A` - Success Green):** Delivered stock, verified batch releases, active compliance accreditations, and healthy metrics.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances warm authority with dense, clinical data scannability.

### Primary Typographic Roles
- **Page Titles Exclusively:** Newsreader semi-bold (28/34) is reserved strictly for primary page titles, hospital dashboard welcome headers, and high-level section titles. It conveys prestige and institutional stature without interfering with operational data.
- **Operational & System Text:** Inter drives the entirety of the portal interface—navigation menus, metrics, data grids, modals, form controls, and statuses.

### Data & Financial Standards
- **Tabular Numerals:** All data grids, batch quantities, unit dosages, and INR amounts (`₹`) must enforce `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1` to guarantee vertical column alignment across thousands of stock lines.
- **Hierarchy of Scale:**
  - Page Titles: 28px/34px (Newsreader Semi-Bold)
  - KPI & Metric Figures: 28px/32px (Inter Semi-Bold with tabular figures)
  - Section Headings: 18px/24px (Inter Semi-Bold)
  - Body Text & Primary Form Values: 14px/20px (Inter Regular/Medium)
  - Data Table Cells & Supporting Meta: 12px/16px (Inter Regular/Medium)
  - Badges & Micro Statuses: 11px/14px (Inter Medium uppercase or title case)

## Layout & Spacing

The portal layout adheres to a fixed-fluid hybrid structure optimized for dashboard utility, rapid data review, and clinical workflow continuity.

### Master Screen Architecture
- **Persistent Left Navigation:** Fixed-width 260px Deep Teal (`#164E52`) sidebar. Remains pinned on desktop, collapsing to an off-canvas drawer on screens below 1024px.
- **Top Utility Bar:** Pinned 64px crisp white header spanning the content area. Houses the partner hospital identity/logo anchor on the left, and the dedicated Account Manager card on the right, bordered below by 1px Muted Sand.
- **Content Canvas:** Fluid area operating on a 12-column responsive layout with 24px (`space-lg`) gutters and 32px (`space-xl`) desktop margins.
- **Persistent Right Support Panel:** 320px contextual rail featuring Mint Mist (`#DCE9E1`) support assistance, order guidelines, and hotline contact info, collapsable on compact viewports.

### Rhythm & Sizing
- Spacing relies on a clean 8px baseline rhythm (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px, `space-xl` = 32px).
- Internal card padding is standardized at 24px (`space-lg`) for analytical views and 16px (`space-md`) for high-density clinical registries.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual separation across the institutional portal is achieved through crisp 1px borders, subtle tonal layering, and minimal ambient diffusion rather than deep dropped shadows.

### Border-First Flat Stacking
- **Standard Surfaces:** Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`) containers float over the Soft Ivory (`#F7F5EF`) canvas purely through 1px solid Muted Sand (`#D8CBB8`) boundaries. Default cards use zero shadow (`box-shadow: none`) to maintain an uncluttered, document-grade feel.
- **Interactive Hover Elevation:** Interactive metric tiles and table rows gain immediate responsiveness through a microscopic border color change to Deep Teal (`#164E52`) and a quiet ambient lift: `0 2px 8px rgba(22, 78, 82, 0.06)`.
- **Top Bar & Panels:** The 64px Top Bar is anchored by a bottom 1px Muted Sand rule. The Mint Mist persistent help panel utilizes a left 1px Muted Sand border flush with the viewport.
- **Floating Modals & Flyouts:** Clinical order confirmation modals and critical dispensing overrides utilize a structured containment outline with a restrained ambient backdrop: `0 16px 32px rgba(22, 78, 82, 0.12)`, paired with a tinted backdrop overlay of `rgba(43, 47, 46, 0.40)`.

## Shapes

The geometric personality balances clinical architecture with ergonomic softness.

### Shape Geometry Rules
- **Clinical Cards & Data Modules:** Standardized strictly at 16px corner radius (`rounded-lg` token variant) for all content cards, KPI groupings, and contextual assistance panels.
- **Interactive Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Selects):** Bounded by an 8px radius (`rounded`) to provide solid tactile affordance without feeling bubbly or informal.
- **Status Chips & KPI Tags:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for immediate recognition as non-editable categorization tokens.
- **Persistent Utility Elements:** The Account Manager card within the top bar utilizes an 8px corner radius with an embedded circular avatar (36px diameter).

## Components

### Top Utility Bar (64px)
- **Container:** Pure `#FFFFFF` background, exactly 64px height, full-width with a 1px solid `#D8CBB8` bottom border.
- **Partner Hospital Identity:** Left-aligned area featuring partner institution mark and hospital name set in 14px Inter Semi-Bold (`#2B2F2E`).
- **Account Manager Card:** Right-aligned interactive container (height 44px, padding 4px 12px, 8px radius, 1px `#D8CBB8` border). Contains a 36px circular avatar, Manager Name (12px Inter Semi-Bold), direct contact ext (11px Inter `#4F7A92`), and a dedicated 1-click dial/message trigger.

### Navigation Sidebar (Deep Teal)
- **Container:** `#164E52` solid fill with a 3px–4px `#5B3A57` (Plum) horizontal accent rule at the very top edge.
- **Navigation Items:** Minimum 44px height tap targets with 12px horizontal padding.
- **Typography & Icons:** Inactive items set in Soft Ivory at 80% opacity (`rgba(247, 245, 239, 0.8)`).
- **Active State:** Solid Soft Ivory (`#F7F5EF`) icon and text, accompanied by a 3px left active indicator bar in Plum (`#5B3A57`) and a subtle background highlight of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.

### Buttons & Touch Targets
- **Tap Sizing:** Strict minimum 44px height across all interactive elements (buttons, segmented controls, table row actions).
- **Primary Action:** Deep Teal (`#164E52`) fill, `#FFFFFF` text, 8px radius. Hover shifts to `#1e6368` (200ms ease).
- **Secondary Action:** 1px Muted Sand (`#D8CBB8`) border, transparent background, Charcoal (`#2B2F2E`) text. Hover introduces Mint Mist (`#DCE9E1`) at 40% fill.
- **Destructive/Emergency Action:** Terracotta (`#B4552F`) fill, `#FFFFFF` text. Used for aborting shipments or raising quality batch recalls.
- **Focus Rings:** Unbroken 2px outline in Saffron (`#E3A72F`) with a 2px offset.

### Metric Badges, Chips & Statuses
- **KPI Metrics:** Dusty Blue (`#4F7A92`) icon fill accompanied by metric counts formatted in Inter Semi-Bold tabular figures.
- **Clinical Order Status Chips:** Height 28px, 9999px radius, padding 4px 10px, 12px Inter Medium.
  - *Pending / In-Transit:* Background `rgba(227, 167, 47, 0.15)`, text Charcoal (`#2B2F2E`), with a 6px Saffron (`#E3A72F`) dot.
  - *Critical Alert / Delayed:* Background `rgba(180, 85, 47, 0.12)`, text Terracotta (`#B4552F`), with a 6px Terracotta dot.
  - *Fulfilled / Released:* Background `rgba(63, 125, 90, 0.12)`, text Success Green (`#3F7D5A`), with a 6px Green dot.

### Persistent Help Panel (Mint Mist)
- **Container:** Mint Mist (`#DCE9E1`) background fill, 16px corner radius, internal padding 20px.
- **Content:** Institutional Support Hotline direct-dial link (44px target), Clinical Pharmacist desk hours, instant batch lookup tool, and active protocol reminders. Bordered by 1px solid `rgba(22, 78, 82, 0.15)`.

### Data Grids & Clinical Order Tables
- **Header:** Background Soft Ivory (`#F7F5EF`), 12px Inter Semi-Bold uppercase in Charcoal (`#2B2F2E`) at 70% opacity, bottom 1px Muted Sand border.
- **Rows:** Minimum height 48px to satisfy touch requirements, crisp `#FFFFFF` background, alternating hover state of `rgba(220, 233, 225, 0.3)` (subtle Mint Mist tint).
- **Cell Content:** Text 14px Inter Regular (`#2B2F2E`), tabular figures for quantity counts, batch identifiers, and INR pricing (`₹`).
