Our Frontline Journey

From emergency response to everyday clinical precision.

The clinical trial-by-fire that redefined how we formulate medical yarns, seams, and fit geometry.

MARCH 2020

PPE Kits & Emergency Medical Clothing

Converted three high-speed textile units in 72 hours to supply 2,40,000 SITRA-cleared protective barrier suits to frontline government and tertiary hospital ICUs across India during peak critical supply disruption.

OCTOBER 2020

Community Mask Distribution Drives

Partnered with civic healthcare bodies to engineer reusable 4-layer antimicrobial botanical masks, donating over 650,000 units to municipal sanitation staff and rural primary health centers across southern states.

AUGUST 2021

First Hospital Uniform Fleet Order

Awarded nationwide clinical outfitting contract for premier Apollo and Aster DM hospital networks after multi-stage trials demonstrated zero fabric breakdown and zero seam tear across 200 high-temperature autoclave cycles.

JANUARY 2023 – PRESENT

Velcura Brand Launch

Launched Velcura as Vastram's dedicated botanical clinical direct-to-clinician and institutional brand, merging proprietary Ayur-Flex™ 4-way stretch mechanics with therapeutic herbal dye infusions.
