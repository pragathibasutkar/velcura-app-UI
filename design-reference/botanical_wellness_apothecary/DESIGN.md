## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a calm, botanical, clinical-wellness aesthetic tailored for modern Indian restorative health and preventive medicine. It fuses traditional Ayurvedic sensibilities with contemporary clinical precision, presenting an aura of mindful luxury, clinical transparency, and organic purity.

### Target Audience & Emotional Arc
- **Target Audience:** Conscientious modern wellness seekers in India seeking high-potency herbal formulations, clean clinical transparency, and grounded lifestyle rituals.
- **Emotional Tone:** Restorative, grounding, immaculate, and scientifically credible. Interfaces avoid chaotic pharmaceutical e-commerce noise in favor of serene editorial spaces, generous breathing room, and botanical serenity.

### Visual Style
- **Modern Botanical Minimalism:** Expansive soft-toned backdrops paired with deliberate typographic hierarchy, fine organic line illustrations, and structured clinical layouts.
- **Organic Depth:** Micro-interactions and subtle teal-tinted ambient diffusions evoke tactile glass apothecary bottles and high-end natural paper stock.

## Colors

The palette balances restorative botanical tones with clinical depths and warm natural neutrals. Contrast requirements must be maintained across all wellness and commercial actions.

### Core Canvas & Structure
- **Primary (`#164E52` - Deep Teal):** Primary interactive touchpoints, main brand actions, editorial headlines, and key navigation highlights.
- **Secondary (`#0E3538` - Teal Night):** Immersive CTA highlight ribbons, global navigational header bases, and the global storefront footer.
- **Tertiary (`#A7B8A1` - Sage Green):** Botanical motifs, delicate tinted chip backgrounds, active step markers, and organic stroke artwork.
- **Neutral Page Surface (`#F7F5EF` - Soft Ivory):** Primary global canvas background. Provides an organic, non-glare, tactile foundation.
- **Container Surface (`#FFFFFF` - Crisp White):** Product containers, elevated cards, prescription uploads, and clinical review panels.

### Structural Supports & Feedback
- **Mint Mist (`#DCE9E1`):** Informational callout containers, dosage reminders, and botanical ingredient breakdown surfaces.
- **Muted Sand (`#D8CBB8`):** Form controls borders, subtle horizontal architectural rules, and metadata dividers.
- **Charcoal (`#2B2F2E`):** Universal high-legibility body, form labels, and critical transactional text on Ivory or White surfaces.

### Functional Accents (Strict ≤10% visual surface allocation)
- **Terracotta (`#B4552F`):** Urgency tags, low inventory alerts, flash wellness offers, and high-visibility secondary actions on dark backgrounds.
- **Saffron (`#E3A72F`):** Editorial highlight underlines (4px), customer satisfaction star ratings, "Bestseller" badges, and global accessibility focus indicators.
- **Dusty Blue (`#4F7A92`):** Ayush / GMP clinical certifications, lab test validations, and pharmacological verification seals.
- **Plum (`#5B3A57`):** Pure extracts, reserve apothecary tiers, and bespoke luxury wellness category badges.

### Color Accessibility Guardrails
- Never set white typography on Sage Green (`#A7B8A1`) or Saffron (`#E3A72F`). Always pair with Charcoal (`#2B2F2E`).
- Body text remains consistently Charcoal (`#2B2F2E`) over Ivory or White for sustained reading comfort.

## Typography

The typographical pairing unites editorial botanical heritage with pristine functional legibility.

### Headline Expression
- **Newsreader:** Employed for all primary display, promotional, and category headlines. Its high-style editorial serif curves evoke timeless wellness journals, literary precision, and vintage apothecary labels.
- **Accents:** Key section headlines frequently pair with an organic 4px underline in Saffron (`#E3A72F`) with a 12px vertical separation to anchor section narrative beats.

### Functional Typography
- **Inter:** Drives all product attributes, nutritional/dosage breakdowns, transactional data, form fields, and navigation elements. Provides maximum structural clarity across desktop and mobile devices.
- **Monetary Figures:** Use Inter Medium or Semi-Bold for INR pricing (`₹`) to ensure clean tabular alignment in shopping carts, ingredient weight ratios, and checkout summaries.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an editorial 12-column grid designed for spacious storytelling, high-end botanical product showcases, and structured clinical diagnostics.

### Grid Architecture
- **Max Content Width:** 1280px, centered horizontally on viewport screens above 1440px.
- **Columns:** 12-column symmetrical layout on desktop; collapses to 6 columns on tablet (768px - 1024px) and 2 or 4 columns on mobile viewports (<768px).
- **Gutters:** Standardized at 24px (`1.5rem`) across all breakpoints to ensure rhythmic consistency.

### Vertical Cadence
- **Desktop Sections:** 96px (`6rem` / `space-3xl`) vertical padding separates distinct marketing, editorial, and merchandising tiers.
- **Mobile Sections:** Collapses to 56px (`3.5rem` / `space-2xl`) vertical section padding to maintain density without sacrificing brand serenity.
- **Base Unit:** Grounded strictly on an 8px architectural grid system (`4px` for tight inline badges, `8px`, `16px`, `24px`, `32px`, `48px`, `64px`, `96px`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy prioritizes tactile paper-like surfaces and ambient botanical light over steep drop-shadows or stark borders.

### Surface Tiers
- **Canvas Base:** Soft Ivory (`#F7F5EF`) sits as the foundational plane.
- **Interactive Container Plane:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) cards float softly above the canvas using a specialized botanical shadow.
- **Immersion Bands:** Teal Night (`#0E3538`) surfaces sit flush, drawing visual focus to promotional tiers, newsletter consultation prompts, or global checkout bands.

### Shadow Characteristics
- **Botanical Card Shadow:** `0 8px 24px rgba(22, 78, 82, 0.10)`. Shadows are deeply diffused and tinted with the primary Deep Teal pigment rather than black or gray.
- **Elevated Hover Elevation:** On hover, product cards translate vertically by `-2px` while transitioning shadow to `0 12px 32px rgba(22, 78, 82, 0.14)`.
- **Modals & Overlays:** `0 24px 48px rgba(14, 53, 56, 0.22)`, accompanied by a subtle backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)` over `rgba(14, 53, 56, 0.40)`).

## Shapes

The shape system blends clinical structure with botanical curvature.

### Geometry Specifications
- **Interactive Controls & Inputs:** 10px corner radius (`rounded-[10px]`) balances modern ergonomical comfort with functional stability.
- **Cards & Content Blocks:** 12px corner radius (`rounded-[12px]`) provides comfortable framing for product photography, formulation details, and clinical benefits.
- **Photography & Hero Showcase:** 20px corner radius (`rounded-[20px]`) softens lifestyle imagery, botanical macro-shots, and raw herb harvesting visuals.
- **Chips, Badges, & Pills:** Fully rounded (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for ingredient tags, cert badges, and micro tags.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Height & Radius:** Fixed 52px height with 10px corner radius across primary actions.
- **Typography:** 15px Inter Semi-Bold (`label-lg`) with centered optical alignment.
- **Primary Button:** Background Deep Teal (`#164E52`), text White (`#FFFFFF`). Hover shifts to `#1c5f64` with 200ms ease-out.
- **Secondary CTA (On Dark Bands):** Background Terracotta (`#B4552F`), text White (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Ghost / Outlined Button:** 1px border Muted Sand (`#D8CBB8`), text Charcoal (`#2B2F2E`), background transparent. On hover, background shifts to Sage Green at 15% opacity (`rgba(167, 184, 161, 0.15)`).

### Input Fields & Selects
- **Height & Structure:** 52px tall, 10px radius, bounded by a 1px border of Muted Sand (`#D8CBB8`).
- **Internal Canvas:** Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`) with Charcoal (`#2B2F2E`) input value text.
- **Focus Protocol:** Strict 3px solid Saffron (`#E3A72F`) outer focus ring with 1px offset, transitioning smoothly over 200ms ease-out.

### Product & Editorial Cards
- **Construction:** Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`) surface, 12px radius, botanical tinted shadow (`0 8px 24px rgba(22, 78, 82, 0.10)`).
- **Interaction:** Hover triggers a clean `-2px` Y-axis lift and intensified deep-teal shadow over 250ms ease-out.
- **Imagery:** Product crops utilize 20px inner radius bounds with neutral organic backdrops.

### Chips & Metadata Tags
- **Geometry:** Full pill shape (height 28px - 32px, padding horizontal 14px).
- **Ingredient / Botanical Chips:** Sage Green tint background (`#A7B8A1` at 25% opacity), text Charcoal (`#2B2F2E`). Never white text on Sage.
- **Bestseller & New Tags:** Background Saffron (`#E3A72F`), text Charcoal (`#2B2F2E`).
- **Sale & Urgency Tags:** Background Terracotta (`#B4552F`), text White (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Certifications (Ayush / GMP):** Background Dusty Blue tint (`#4F7A92` at 15% opacity), text Dusty Blue (`#4F7A92`) or Charcoal (`#2B2F2E`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Geometry:** Checkboxes use 4px corner radius; radios use pure circular geometry.
- **State Styling:** Inactive border 1.5px Muted Sand (`#D8CBB8`). Active state fills with Deep Teal (`#164E52`) featuring crisp white check/dot glyphs.

### Iconography & Illustrations
- **Icon Set:** Thin-line icons (e.g., Lucide) rendered strictly at 1.5px stroke width.
- **Tone:** Delicate, monoline, organic botanicals (leaves, seeds, drop pipettes, clinical crosses) matching the active text tone or Deep Teal (`#164E52`).

### Specialized Content Modules
- **Informational & Tip Panels:** Mint Mist (`#DCE9E1`) background with 12px radius, 16px internal padding, and 1.5px Deep Teal line accents for dosage guidelines, ritual pacing, and clinical contraindications.
- **Pricing Expression:** Standardized realistic Indian currency formatting (`₹2,499`, `₹850`) set in Inter Semi-Bold with subtle MRP comparison tags struck out in Muted Sand.
