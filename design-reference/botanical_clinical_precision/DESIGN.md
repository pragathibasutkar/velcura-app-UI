## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a refined, botanical-clinical sensibility designed for healthcare administration, supply-chain monitoring, and Ayurvedic clinical governance. The mood balances dense data throughput with airy calm, leveraging an earthy yet structured institutional framework.

The style unites contemporary corporate clarity with physical stationery motifs:
- **Tonal Balance:** Grounded by an assertive dark teal navigation structural spine, framed by warm mineral surfaces, and illuminated by targeted herbal accents.
- **Architectural Rhythm:** Crisp, border-defined planar containers without heavy elevation shadows, giving operational screens an organized ledger quality.
- **Audience:** Operations directors, hospital administrators, clinical auditors, and pharmaceutical procurement teams who require low eye-strain during prolonged, high-density analytical sessions.

## Colors

The palette establishes clinical trust, historical grounding, and high-readability data states through botanical-mineral pigmentation.

### Structural Foundations
- **Primary Brand / Key Action:** Deep Teal (`#164E52`) is the primary driver for high-priority CTA buttons, active selection pills, hyperlinked identifiers, and core chart series.
- **Dark Structural Canvas:** Teal Night (`#0E3538`) acts as the anchor for the fixed primary sidebar navigation rail, persistent utility bars, and contrast footers.
- **Page Canvas:** Soft Ivory (`#F7F5EF`) replaces sterile cold white with a warmer, daylight-adjusted baseline that diminishes eye fatigue.
- **Surface Containers:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) isolates cards, modular sheets, dialogs, and table records against the canvas.
- **Text & Foreground:** Charcoal (`#2B2F2E`) ensures contrast across all light surfaces for data labels, dense prose, and metrics.
- **Borders & Dividers:** Muted Sand (`#D8CBB8`) acts as the unifying 1px separator for structural lines, card rims, and table dividers.

### Contextual & Status Roles
- **Success:** Emerald Herb (`#3F7D5A`) with a 12% tint background (`#3F7D5A1F`) for verified shipments, valid clinical records, and positive inventory shifts.
- **Warning / Pending:** Amber Resin (`#B7791F`) with a 12% tint background (`#B7791F1F`) for queued batches, compliance reviews, and stock warnings.
- **Error / Critical:** Iron Oxide (`#B3261E`) with a 12% tint background (`#B3261E1F`) for regulatory alerts, failed integrations, and stockouts.
- **Info / Institutional:** Dusty Blue (`#4F7A92`) with a 12% tint background (`#4F7A921F`) for system audit logs, clinical tag metadata, and partner badges.

### Specialty & Domain Tints
- **Active Accent & Indicators:** Saffron (`#E3A72F`) provides navigation focus lines, active state markers, and interactive focus outlines.
- **Botanical Fillers:** Sage Green (`#A7B8A1`) and Mint Mist (`#DCE9E1`) provide soft chart fills and data table row hover states.
- **Urgent Action:** Terracotta (`#B4552F`) strictly flags imminent material shortages.
- **Enterprise Tier:** Plum (`#5B3A57`) highlights premium institutional procurement tiers.

### Data Visualization Sequence
For multidimensional charts, data series must adhere strictly to:
1. Deep Teal (`#164E52`)
2. Sage Green (`#A7B8A1`)
3. Dusty Blue (`#4F7A92`)
4. Saffron (`#E3A72F`)
5. Terracotta (`#B4552F`)
6. Plum (`#5B3A57`)

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial balance between classical authority and utilitarian analytical performance.

- **Primary Display & Landmark Headings:** Expressive serif typography is reserved strictly for level-1 operational views and page titles (`headline-lg`). This adds historical botanical pedigree to administrative views.
- **Section Headers & Module Titles:** Inter semi-bold (`headline-sm`, `headline-md`) provides clear, neutral structure for high-density cards, panel groupings, and dashboard summaries.
- **Operational Figures & Tables:** Inter is paired with mandatory tabular numeral formatting (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`). Any numerical currency, inventory quantity, batch ratio, or percentage metric must align predictably down column lines.
- **Badges & Microcopy:** Clean uppercase or mixed-case tracking on 11px and 12px levels ensures legible clinical classifications at diminutive sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

The application shell uses a fixed operational canvas model engineered for wide-screen desktop efficiency while degrading gracefully to tablet and mobile form factors.

### Operational Application Shell
- **Primary Sidebar:** Fixed at `264px` width on desktop screens, housing navigation tiers with quick-collapse capability down to a `72px` icon rail. Uses Teal Night background with an absolute active indicator anchored to the left or right of selected nav items.
- **Persistent Header Bar:** Fixed `64px` height, anchored to the top of the viewport across the content pane with a bottom border of 1px Muted Sand.
- **Main Canvas:** Soft Ivory background with consistent `24px` (`space-lg`) internal viewport padding across administrative screens.

### Grid Engine
- **Desktop (1024px+):** Fluid 12-column grid with `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters and outer margins. Panels typically span 3, 4, 6, 8, or 12 columns.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 6-column grid with `1rem` (`16px`) gutters. Side rail automatically folds into the 72px rail mode.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid stack with `1rem` margins; the sidebar shifts off-canvas behind an overlay drawer.

### Spatial Rhythm
Density must remain predictable across forms and grids. Component interiors strictly honor the 4px base increment (`0.25rem` multipliers). Multi-metric cards utilize `1.5rem` internal padding for titles with `1rem` internal spacing for data grids.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids floating drop shadows, blurred ambient lighting, and skeuomorphic drop effects. Depth is established structurally through planar tiering, deliberate background contrasts, and delicate 1px boundary lines.

### Planar Tiering Model
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Soft Ivory (`#F7F5EF`). The grounded foundation on which all interactive panels rest.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Layer):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) surrounded by a crisp, continuous 1px outline in Muted Sand (`#D8CBB8`). No drop shadow is applied in normal states.
- **Level 2 (Active/Hover Layer):** Interacting with actionable cards introduces a subtle tinted outline transition to Deep Teal (`#164E52`) or an ultra-crisp, zero-spread offset border rather than a diffuse shadow.
- **Level 3 (Overlay / Modal / Flyout):** Surface remains Pure White with a 1px border (`#D8CBB8`), backed by a high-contrast dimming scrim using Teal Night at 40% opacity (`rgba(14, 53, 56, 0.40)`). Floating tooltips and contextual popovers rely on a tight `0 4px 12px rgba(14, 53, 56, 0.08)` perimeter containment.

### Border Separators
- Dividers between table cells, segmented tabs, and split headers share an identical 1px thickness calibrated strictly in Muted Sand (`#D8CBB8`).

## Shapes

The geometric framework balances human warmth with data density, utilizing an 8px radius standard across the majority of transactional components.

- **Standard Components (8px / `0.5rem`):** Buttons, text input controls, dropdown selects, table filter groups, and nested inline panels utilize the default 8px curve for ergonomic utility.
- **Structural Card Enclosures (16px / `1rem`):** Content containers, primary reporting cards, KPI summaries, and modal viewports use a 16px corner radius (`rounded-lg`) to soften expansive views.
- **Pills & Metatags (Full Pill / `9999px`):** Status badges, clinical classification chips, and operational count indicators use a fully rounded pill structure to distinguish them from standard interactive buttons and inputs.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** 40px height, 8px radius, Deep Teal (`#164E52`) solid fill, Soft Ivory text, 14px Inter Semi-Bold. Active/hover transitions smoothly (150ms ease-out) to a deeper shade of `#0E3538`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** 40px height, 8px radius, Pure White fill, 1px Muted Sand border, Deep Teal text. Hover shifts background to Mint Mist (`#DCE9E1`).
- **Destructive:** 40px height, 8px radius, Iron Oxide (`#B3261E`) fill or 1px border with `#B3261E` text.

### Form Inputs & Selects
- **Text Fields:** 40px height, 8px radius, Pure White or Soft Ivory background, 1px Muted Sand border, Charcoal 14px body text. 
- **Placeholder:** Charcoal at 50% opacity.
- **Focus Ring:** 3px ring in Saffron (`#E3A72F`) with zero pixel blur (crisp offset).
- **Disabled State:** Opacity at 45% with background defaulting to Soft Ivory.

### Badges, Status Pills & Chips
- **Geometry:** Height 24px, pill-shaped (`9999px`), padding horizontal 10px, Inter 12px font size with medium or semi-bold weight.
- **Status Application:** Always paired with a 12% solid tint background against full-strength foreground glyphs/labels:
  - *Success:* `#3F7D5A` on `#3F7D5A1F`
  - *Warning / Pending:* `#B7791F` on `#B7791F1F`
  - *Error / Alert:* `#B3261E` on `#B3261E1F`
  - *Clinical / Info:* `#4F7A92` on `#4F7A921F`

### Data Tables
- **Row Architecture:** 52px fixed row height, single 1px bottom border in Muted Sand.
- **Row Interaction:** Hover state activates a solid background fill in Mint Mist (`#DCE9E1`) across 150ms ease-out.
- **Headers:** Sticky positioning, subtle Muted Sand translucent tint, 12px Inter semi-bold, uppercase tracking.
- **Numerical Alignment:** Numeric data columns are right-aligned using `tabular-nums`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Checkboxes:** 18px square with a 4px corner radius, 1.5px Muted Sand border on resting states, filled with Deep Teal (`#164E52`) and a White checkmark when checked.
- **Radio Buttons:** 18px circular with Deep Teal inner dot when selected.
- **Focus:** 3px Saffron (`#E3A72F`) focus indicator on keyboard tab navigation.

### Cards & Grouping Surfaces
- **Specification:** Pure White fill, 16px corner radius, 1px border in Muted Sand.
- **Header:** Accommodates Inter Semi-Bold 16px section titles with optional contextual action menus right-aligned.
- **Dividers:** Internal card divisions utilize 1px Muted Sand hairline borders spanning edge to edge.

### System Feedback & Loading
- **Loaders:** Asynchronous data loading cards implement a soft skeleton pulse cycling between Muted Sand at 20% opacity and Soft Ivory.
