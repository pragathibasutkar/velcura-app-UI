How to Take Clinical Anatomical Measures

health_and_safety Standard Cloth Measuring Tape Recommended
01

1. Bust / Torso Circumference

Measure the fullest portion of your chest directly under the armpits. Keep the tape horizontal, firm, and level across shoulder blades without compressing tissue.

Key for: V-neck drape & shoulder reach
02

2. Natural Waistline

Find the narrowest natural point of the torso (typically 2.5 cm above the navel). Measure during normal relaxed exhalation while standing erect with feet shoulder-width.

Key for: Drawstring comfort during bending
03

3. Full Hip & Inseam

Measure fullest seat circumference with heels together. For scrub pants or cargo joggers, verify the inner inseam from perineum down to the ankle malleolus bone.

Key for: Stool seating & squat freedom
lightbulb

Clinician Pro-Tip: If you plan to wear technical base layers or thermal innerwear during cold OT rounds, add 1.5 cm to your bust circumference prior to selecting your matrix size.
